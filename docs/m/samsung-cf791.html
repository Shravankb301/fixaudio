Will It DDC?

Does the Samsung CF791 support volume control on a Mac?

Yes, with the right tool. The Samsung CF791 accepts DDC volume commands, but lies when asked about its current state. Apps that read before writing will look broken; a write-only tool works fine.

Also sold as C34F791.
